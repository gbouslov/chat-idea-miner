# write-report

Write for the owner, who will skim. Headings that say the point, short bullets, numbers over adjectives, no filler.

## out/IDEAS.md
- One line on top: how many chats were read, from where, and which dates.
- **Do this one first:** the top idea in two lines. Then the evidence: each supporting chat by date and source, with the owner's exact quote.
- Ideas 2 and 3 in the same shape, shorter.
- **Wishes:** ideas the owner keeps coming back to that nobody has asked for yet, each with its chat count. Kind and plain.

## out/WHO-WANTS-IT.md
For each of the top 3: the posts (who, when, link, their words, why it fits), what they use now and where it falls short, and the first move.

## out/INSIGHTS.md
Each lesson with its result, the owner's words and the date. Mark the ones worth posting publicly, written as the owner would post them, never naming a client or a private person.

## out/SKILLS.md
Each workflow that came back 3 or more times: how often, the dates, and a 5-line draft of the skill that would do it.

## Then
Tell the user in five lines or fewer: the idea to do first, why, the first person to reply to, and where the files are.
